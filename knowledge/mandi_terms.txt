AGRICULTURAL MARKET TERMINOLOGY


MANDI
-----

A mandi is a wholesale agricultural market where agricultural
commodities are brought for trading.


MODAL PRICE
-----------

Modal price represents the prevailing or representative
reported price of a commodity in a market.

For actual modal price values, the system must query the
PostgreSQL database.


MINIMUM PRICE
-------------

Minimum price represents the lowest reported price for a
commodity during the relevant market observation period.


MAXIMUM PRICE
-------------

Maximum price represents the highest reported price for a
commodity during the relevant market observation period.


ARRIVAL
-------

Arrival refers to agricultural produce reaching a mandi
during a particular period.


ARRIVAL VOLUME
--------------

Arrival volume represents the quantity of agricultural
produce reaching a mandi.

The project expresses arrival volume in Metric Tons.


WHOLESALE MARKET
----------------

A wholesale agricultural market is a marketplace where
agricultural commodities are traded in larger quantities.


PRICE DISCREPANCY
-----------------

A price discrepancy occurs when the same agricultural
commodity has different reported prices across different
markets.


WEATHER DISRUPTION
------------------

Weather conditions such as heavy rainfall or extreme heat
can affect harvesting, transportation, supply and market
arrivals.

Such explanations provide contextual knowledge. Actual
weather observations and actual market price changes must
be retrieved from the database.
