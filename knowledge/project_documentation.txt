PROJECT DOCUMENTATION

PROJECT TITLE
-------------

Agri-Market Intelligence and Price Tracker:
A BI Solution for Indian Wholesale Mandis


BUSINESS PROBLEM
----------------

The agricultural supply chain contains fragmented market
information.

Farmers, traders and logistics providers may need to compare
prices, supply volumes, locations and weather conditions
across agricultural markets.


KEY BUSINESS QUESTIONS
----------------------

1. What is the price of a crop in a particular mandi?

2. Which mandi has the highest or lowest price?

3. What is the average price across markets?

4. How much crop is arriving at a mandi?

5. How do prices differ between districts?

6. What weather conditions were observed at a market?

7. How are price and weather observations related?


DATA WAREHOUSE
--------------

The system uses a Star Schema.

Central fact table:

fact_mandi_daily

Dimension tables:

dim_location
dim_crop
dim_weather
dim_date


BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE
---------------------

The warehouse can be connected to BI tools for dashboards
and interactive analysis.

The conversational AI layer provides a natural-language
interface over the structured warehouse and knowledge base.


AI LAYER
--------

The AI layer consists of:

1. Query Router
2. SQL Agent
3. RAG Retriever
4. Hybrid Answer Generator


SQL AGENT
---------

The SQL Agent handles structured questions requiring actual
database observations.

Examples:

- highest onion price
- average tomato price
- crop arrivals
- market comparisons
- rainfall measurements
- date-based analysis


RAG
---

RAG handles unstructured knowledge.

Examples:

- definitions
- agricultural terminology
- project documentation
- explanations of business concepts
- explanations of the Star Schema


HYBRID
------

Hybrid queries require both structured database information
and contextual knowledge.

For example:

"Why did onion prices increase after heavy rainfall?"

The SQL system can provide the observed prices and weather
measurements.

The knowledge base can provide contextual information about
how rainfall may affect harvesting, transportation and supply.


IMPORTANT
---------

The AI must not invent actual market observations.

Numerical observations should come from the database.

Contextual explanations should come from the knowledge base.
