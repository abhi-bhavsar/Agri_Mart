AGRI-MARKET INTELLIGENCE DATA DICTIONARY

PROJECT:
Agri-Market Intelligence and Price Tracker

PURPOSE:
This knowledge base describes the meaning of the agricultural
market warehouse and its major business concepts.

FACT TABLE
-----------

fact_mandi_daily

The central fact table stores daily agricultural market
observations.

Typical measures include:

- minimum price
- maximum price
- modal price
- crop arrival volume
- market-related daily measurements


LOCATION DIMENSION
------------------

dim_location

Contains geographical information.

Typical attributes:

- state
- district
- market name


CROP DIMENSION
--------------

dim_crop

Contains agricultural commodity information.

Typical attributes:

- crop name
- crop category


DATE DIMENSION
--------------

dim_date

Contains date and seasonal information.

Typical attributes:

- date
- day
- month
- year
- season


WEATHER DIMENSION
-----------------

dim_weather

Contains weather observations associated with market
locations and dates.

Typical attributes may include:

- temperature
- rainfall
- weather conditions


BUSINESS METRICS
----------------

MINIMUM PRICE

The lowest reported price for a commodity during the
relevant market observation period.


MAXIMUM PRICE

The highest reported price for a commodity during the
relevant market observation period.


MODAL PRICE

The representative or prevailing reported price of a
commodity in a market.


ARRIVAL VOLUME

The quantity of agricultural produce arriving at a mandi.

In this project, crop volumes are represented in
Metric Tons.


IMPORTANT DATA RULE
-------------------

Actual numerical observations such as prices, rainfall,
temperatures, arrival volumes and dates must be retrieved
from PostgreSQL.

Definitions and business terminology can be retrieved
from the knowledge base.
